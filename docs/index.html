01 / THE QUESTION

What happens
between two songs?

The interesting part is the music they can make together.

A familiar groove holds. A new texture appears. The foundation changes hands. An incoming phrase arrives with room to breathe. LADIO describes those moments separately, then builds a shared transport and an effect graph around them.

Apple Music AutoMix is the product reference. LADIO is an independent implementation, with its own planner and native DSP. It does not include Apple frameworks, private presets or Apple model weights.

04 / SHARED TIME

Move together.
Arrive with intent.

Compatible sources follow one smooth tempo evolution. B can enter at A’s pulse and reach its native rate at the planned arrival. Independent clips participate in that timing.

For incompatible pulses, the planner considers a bounded musical bridge rather than forcing an extreme continuous stretch.

source rate = shared pulse / source pulse

Illustrative 1:1 example. Half/double-time interpretation is considered separately; it does not double the recording speed.

05 / A NATIVE EFFECT GRAPH

Give the sound
somewhere to go.

01

Spectral movement

High-pass, low-pass, EQ and wetness evolve independently of the deck fader.

02

Echo & space

Tempo delay and room/reverb have separate feed and return curves. A tail can outlive the dry source.

03

Motion & repetition

Measured holds, staged repeats, flanging and rhythmic gates create development around the arrival.

06 / BOUNDED COMPUTE

Listen first.
Separate when justified.

The public automatic path works with stereo sources. It does not run Whisper or a separator on every song. Provided stem windows are an extension, rather than a fixed preparation tax.

One redistributable model ships in this release: Beat This small0, trained by its upstream authors and integrated through an FP32 ONNX export.

07 / EVIDENCE, NOT A SCORE

Built to be examined.

What can be checked

Source-map continuity, complete incoming attacks, supplied phrase protection, recovery, independent loops/captures, effect-tail reconstruction after seek, allocation-free callback checks and model hashes.

Read the release validation record ↗

What remains empirical

Perceptual quality on new songs, ambiguous beat grids, phone thermal behavior and preparation deadlines. A synthetic test is not a listening study. This release does not claim Apple parity or a universal 20-second budget.
